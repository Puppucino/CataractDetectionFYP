Cataract Surgery FAQ

Q: Is cataract surgery safe?
A: Yes, cataract surgery is one of the safest and most common surgeries performed worldwide. Complications are rare, and most people experience improved vision.

Q: How long does the surgery take?
A: The procedure usually takes less than 30 minutes and is performed on an outpatient basis.

Q: Will I need glasses after surgery?
A: Many people still need reading glasses or glasses for certain activities, but your vision will be much clearer after surgery.

Q: Is the surgery painful?
A: Most patients experience little to no pain during or after the procedure. Numbing drops are used to keep you comfortable.
